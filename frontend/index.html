What are Learning Differences? 🤔

📖

Dyslexia

Difficulty with reading, spelling, and word recognition

🔢

Dyscalculia

Challenges with numbers, math concepts, and calculations

✏️

Dysgraphia

Difficulties with writing, handwriting, and expressing thoughts on paper

🌟 Remember: Every child is unique and special! 🌟

Learning differences don't define intelligence. They just mean children learn in different ways!
