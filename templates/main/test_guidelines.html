Assessment Guidelines

Please read the following instructions before starting the assessment

Important Instructions

Time Requirements
  • The test contains 50 questions
  • Estimated completion time: 10-15 minutes
  • Take your time - there's no rush
Question Types
  • Questions 1-11: Dyslexia indicators
  • Questions 12-22: Dysgraphia indicators
  • Questions 23-35: Dyscalculia indicators
  • Questions 36-50: Psychological impact
How to Answer
  • Each question has 3 options:
  • Yes - This applies most of the time
  • Sometimes - This applies occasionally
  • No - This rarely or never applies
Tips for Accuracy
  • Think about the child's typical behavior
  • Consider recent observations (last 6 months)
  • Be honest and objective in your responses
  • Don't overthink - go with your first instinct
Important: This screening tool is for educational purposes only and does not provide a medical diagnosis. If results suggest potential learning difficulties, please consult with qualified professionals for comprehensive evaluation.
Privacy: All responses are confidential and stored securely. Your data will not be shared with third parties.
